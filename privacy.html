Privacy Policy

FitSync stores account and fitness-plan data in Supabase so your account can sync across devices. FitSync uses Row Level Security so authenticated users can access only their own profile data.

Ami sends only the minimum context needed for a fitness answer to the configured AI provider. Sensitive onboarding fields such as health notes, smoking and alcohol responses are not sent to Ami by the production API.

Do not use FitSync as a medical service. Consult a qualified professional for medical conditions, injuries or symptoms.
