NUTRITION 101

How FitSync estimates your targets

Calories

Estimated from your profile, activity and goal. A target is a planning estimate, not a diagnosis.

Protein

Higher-protein goals support muscle repair; FitSync uses a simple weight-based planning range.

Healthy fats

Fats are energy-dense and important for normal body functions, so the plan keeps them in balance.

Fiber & water

Fiber supports digestion; hydration needs vary with body size, climate and activity.
